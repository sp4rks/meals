Lemony chicken & crispy potatoes

The sort of dinner that looks after itself.

Edit recipe
your photo goes here
Prep 10 minCook 30 minServes 2

What you’ll need

  • 2chicken thigh fillets
  • 400 gbaby potatoes, halved
  • 1lemon, half sliced, half juiced
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 2 sprigsrosemary, leaves picked
  • 1 pinchsalt and black pepper

Let’s make dinner

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Toss the potatoes with half the oil, rosemary and a little salt in a roasting tray.
  2. Add the chicken and lemon slices. Drizzle with the remaining oil and lemon juice, then season.
  3. Roast for 30 minutes, turning the potatoes halfway, until golden and the chicken is cooked through. Check the thickest part reaches 75°C; give it longer if needed.
  4. Rest for 5 minutes. Serve with something green and spoon the lemony tray juices over everything.
A note from our kitchen

Save any extra potatoes for tomorrow’s lunch wraps. Refrigerate leftovers promptly.
